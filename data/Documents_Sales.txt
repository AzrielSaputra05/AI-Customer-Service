## 📄 RENTAL REQUIREMENTS

### Mandatory Requirements
- Original ID card (KTP) (must be valid, no photocopies accepted)
- Original driver's license (SIM C) (must be valid, no photocopies accepted)
- Deposit Rp 500,000 (cash/transfer, 100% refundable)
- Minimum age 17 years old
- Active phone number (WhatsApp)
- Fill in and sign the rental form
- Selfie photo with the motorcycle during handover
- Renter MUST be present in person (cannot be represented)

### Additional Requirements (Conditional)
- Original student ID card (to claim 5% discount)

## ⚠️ PENALTY LIST

### Late Return Penalty
- Late 1 - 2 hours: FREE (grace period)
- Late 2 - 6 hours: Penalty of half the daily rental price
- Late more than 6 hours: Penalty of 1 full day rental price
- Late more than 24 hours: Penalty of 2x the daily rental price
- Late more than 48 hours without confirmation: Penalty of 3x the daily rental price and deposit forfeited

### Late Return Penalty Calculation Examples
- Honda Beat (Rp 75,000/day)
  - Late 2-6 hours: Rp 37,500
  - Late >6 hours: Rp 75,000
  - Late >24 hours: Rp 150,000
  - Late >48 hours: Rp 225,000
- Yamaha NMAX (Rp 125,000/day)
  - Late 2-6 hours: Rp 62,500
  - Late >6 hours: Rp 125,000
  - Late >24 hours: Rp 250,000
  - Late >48 hours: Rp 375,000
- Honda CRF 150L (Rp 150,000/day)

  - Late >24 hours: Rp 300,000
  - Late >48 hours: Rp 450,000
